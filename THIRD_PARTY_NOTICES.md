## Nerd Fonts patch

The file we ship is a Nerd Fonts-patched build (extra glyphs in the Private Use Area, including Powerline separators). Nerd Fonts’ terms are in [assets/fonts/NERD-FONTS-LICENSE.txt](assets/fonts/NERD-FONTS-LICENSE.txt).

Patched fonts remain under OFL 1.1. The Nerd Fonts patcher and related source are MIT, Copyright (c) 2014 Ryan L McIntyre.

Icon glyphs Nerd Fonts merges in come from several projects (Powerline, Font Awesome, Material Design, Octicons, Codicons, and others). Some of those sources use CC BY 4.0 and require attribution of the original icon authors. The Nerd Fonts [license audit](https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/blob/master/license-audit.md) is the inventory of those glyph sets.
